Kitchen Renovation FAQ's

  • How long does a kitchen remodel typically take?

    The length of a kitchen remodel project can vary depending on the size & scope of the renovation. On average, a kitchen remodel can take anywhere from 4 to 12 weeks.

  • How much does a kitchen remodel cost?

    The cost of a kitchen remodel can vary widely depending on the materials, appliances, and labor involved. On average, a kitchen remodel can cost anywhere from $15,000 to $50,000.

  • Do you work with an interior designer?

    Yes, at E&E Remodeling, we have a team of experienced designers who will work with you to create the kitchen of your dreams. Our designers will take into account your vision and budget to create a design plan that meets your needs.

  • Can I still use my kitchen while it's being remodeled?

    It depends on the scope of the renovation. If your kitchen is being completely gutted, you may need to find alternative arrangements for cooking and food storage during the remodel. Our team will work with you to minimize the disruption to your daily routine during the renovation process.

  • What kind of materials do you use for kitchen remodels?

    At E&E Remodeling, we use only the finest materials for our kitchen remodels. We offer a wide range of materials, including cabinetry, countertops, flooring, and appliances, to ensure that our clients have a variety of options to choose from. We source materials from trusted manufacturers to ensure durability and longevity. Additionally, we always stay up-to-date on the latest industry trends and technology to offer cutting-edge materials for our clients.

  • What happens if I change my mind about something during the remodel?

    No problem! Our team at E&E Remodeling is committed to ensuring that our clients are 100% satisfied with the final product. If you change your mind about something during the remodel process, we will work with you to make any necessary changes to ensure that you are completely happy with the end result.

  • Do you offer a warranty for your kitchen remodel services?

    Yes, we offer a comprehensive warranty on all of our kitchen remodel services. Our team is committed to ensuring that our clients are completely satisfied with the end result, and we stand behind our work with a warranty for added peace of mind.
